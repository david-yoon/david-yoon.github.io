<p>
David is a Senior Research Scientist at Adobe Research, specializing in <strong>multimodal understanding</strong> and <strong>natural language processing (NLP)</strong>. His research focuses on advancing long-form text understanding for question answering systems and improving the factual consistency of large language and vision-language models (LLMs/VLMs). More broadly, David is interested in developing AI technologies that address real-world challenges, with a particular emphasis on applications that promote social good.
    
<p> 
<p>
    David received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University in 2020, where he was honored with the Distinguished Dissertation Award. During his doctoral studies, he was advised by <a href="http://milab.snu.ac.kr/kjung/index.html" title="Kyomin Jung">Dr. Kyomin Jung</a>.
</p>
<p>
    Prior to pursuing his Ph.D., David worked as a Staff Software Engineer at the Samsung Research Artificial Intelligence Center from 2006 to 2017. During his tenure, he led and contributed to key initiatives in the engineering and innovation of artificial intelligence and machine learning.
</p>
